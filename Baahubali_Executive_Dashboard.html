Executive Storyline

Baahubali is a franchise-market case, not just a film collection table. The dashboard separates worldwide gross, domestic nett and qualitative buzz indicators so no metric is mixed or overstated.

Data Integrity Notes

Do not mix metric types. Worldwide gross, domestic gross, domestic nett and qualitative buzz are different measures. BB2 30-day territory numbers are domestic nett figures and are separated from worldwide gross. Known-budget ROI excludes budgets that are not reliably public. OTT buzz metrics are marked as qualitative proxies; no private Netflix viewership or unverified X/Twitter volume is fabricated.
Sources are maintained inside the Excel workbook Source_Register sheet. Key source types used: Sacnilk for consistent film gross/budget tables, Times of India citing BoxofficeIndia.com for BB2 30-day domestic nett territory snapshot, Netflix official page for Torchbearer metadata, and media coverage for qualitative buzz/proxy signals.
